How to use

  1. Pasta first — Place the measured dry pasta in the cooker.
  2. Cold water — Add the shown volume. Pasta must be fully covered.
  3. Salt — Add the matching salt (≈ 9 g per litre).
  4. Stir once — Cook uncovered on 1000 W (High / P-HI).
  5. Mid-cook stir — Halfway through, open carefully and stir (especially long pasta).
  6. Test early — Check 10 s before the displayed time. Add 10 s steps to al dente.
  7. Drain — Use the strainer lid. Stand 1 min for better texture.

Algorithm

Fitted for LG NeoChef MH6535 at 1000 W, cold tap ≈ 15 °C, uncovered cooker.

t = t_ad + (1 − γ) · t_heat + t_die + t_geom
  • t_ad — package al dente (min), after a rolling boil
  • t_heat = (V·c_w·ΔT_w + m_p·c_p·ΔT_p) / P_eff
  • c_w = 4.184 J/g·K, ΔT_w = 82 K (15 °C tap → ~97 °C)
  • c_p = 1.8 J/g·K, ΔT_p = 70 K (dry pasta + early soak)
  • P_eff = 650 W (1000 W rating, ~65% coupling in a deep cooker)
  • γ = 0.36 — share of heat-up that already gelatinizes starch
  • t_die = 0.30 min if bronze-cut (slower hydration, less agitation)
  • t_geom — built-ins use −0.15 / 0 / 0.15 / 0.25 min
Result rounded to the nearest 10 s.

Important · Cold tap water only · 1000 W microwave mode only (no grill / combination) · No oil · Contents are very hot.
